Hey there, I'm Eszti — a UX Designer, soon-to-be Full-Stack Developer, Star Wars fan, and Lego enthusiast. I collect skills the way some people collect records: research, interaction design, design systems, frontend development. I like knowing how things work.

I think in systems, and lately I've been building them too. I don't pretend to have every answer on hand, but I'm persistent about finding one.

To me, good design is about understanding people, constraints, and the messy reality of how products actually get made. It means getting to know users, collaborating with stakeholders, navigating technical boundaries, and still finding a solution that serves all of them. Five years in, I've learned how to juggle all of that at once.

Design problems can be chaotic and occasionally maddening, but that's half the fun. They're worth solving.

This past year I've been intentionally stepping into code, completing a full-stack MERN program. The more I learn, the more convinced I am: understanding how products are built makes me a better designer; understanding who they're built for makes me a better developer. That overlap is where I want to keep working.
